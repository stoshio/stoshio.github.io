Frequently Asked

Is my screenplay stored anywhere besides my Mac?
No. SlateBoard saves your work locally on your own Mac, encrypted, and does not upload it anywhere. If you'd like a cloud backup or want to access your screenplay on another Mac, save or move your file into your iCloud Drive folder, macOS will keep it synced for you.

What's the difference between free and Pro?
The free version supports one screenplay up to 30 pages. SlateBoard Pro removes the page limit and unlocks Versioning, Typewriter Mode, Beat Board, and Writing Goals.

Can I import scripts from Final Draft or Fountain?
Yes. SlateBoard supports importing .fdx and Fountain files, and exporting to PDF.
